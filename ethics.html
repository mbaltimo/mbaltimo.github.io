Ethics in IT

IT professionals should reflect on the wider impact of their work and how it supports public good

A computing professional should...

  1. Contribute to society and human well-being, acknowledging that all people are stakeholders in computing
  2. Avoid harm
  3. Be honest and trustworthy
  4. Be far and take action not to discriminate
  5. and more...
